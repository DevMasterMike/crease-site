Crease

A pop-up atlas for iPhone and iPad.

A letter that was never sent folds itself into a tiny paper traveler and walks across the pages of an old pop-up atlas. How far the book is open decides which paper scenery stands up and which lies flat. On a foldable iPhone you fold the phone; everywhere else you drag a bookmark ribbon. Sixty spreads in six chapters, no text in gameplay, no ads, no timers.

The first chapter is free. One purchase opens the rest of the book, for the whole family.

Made by Michael Berinshteyn, Elijah Wellington and Bryson Mazur in Erie, Pennsylvania.
