Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version. Crease does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share information about you. Your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. Apple handles payments, iCloud and Game Center.

This policy explains what the Crease app for iPhone and iPad (the "App") stores, what it sends to Apple, what we receive if you write to us, and the choices you have. It is part of our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

The App is published by Michael Berinshteyn ("we", "us" or "our"), Erie, Pennsylvania, United States, and was made by Michael Berinshteyn, Elijah Wellington and Bryson Mazur. For any personal data described in this policy, we are the controller. You can reach us at berinshteyn@gmail.com.

2. What the App does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your name, email address, birthday or phone number.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not use your information to build a profile of you.
  • It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar or health data.
  • It does not use your information to train artificial intelligence models.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Because of this, the App's privacy label on the App Store is "Data Not Collected".

3. What is stored on your device

To work, the App keeps a small amount of information on your device:

  • Your progress: which pages you have finished, the paper cranes and pressed flowers you have found, the pages on which a hint appeared, whether you have finished the story, and where to reopen the book.
  • Your settings: your choice of control (hinge, ribbon or tilt), Assist, haptics, screen shake, bold outlines and volume levels.
  • Small notes to itself: whether you have already tried the ribbon and the pull-tabs, and which version of these policies you have seen.
  • Whether you own "The rest of the book", which the App checks with Apple each time it opens.

This information stays on your device, protected by iOS. We cannot see it.

4. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so that your other iPhones and iPads can continue where you left off. Apple stores that copy under the Apple Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. Your settings are not synced.

5. Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center, the App reports the achievements you earn and the chapter you are reading (so the Games app can show it and bring you back to it) to Apple's Game Center, where they appear on your Game Center profile. Apple runs Game Center under the Apple Privacy Policy. The App does not store your Game Center identity or send it anywhere else, and it does not read your friends list. You can sign out in Settings › Game Center.

6. Purchases

"The rest of the book" is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your name, Apple Account, payment card or billing address. On your device, the App checks Apple-signed transaction information to know which chapters to open. Apple gives developers sales reports that do not identify individual buyers.

7. Sensors and controls

The App reads the hinge angle on iPhone models that fold and, only when you choose the Tilt control, your device's motion sensors. It also responds to touch, keyboards and game controllers, and plays haptics. All of this happens live on your device. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

8. Age ranges

Some places, including Texas and, as their laws take effect, other U.S. states, require apps to check a user's age range with the app store. In those places the App may ask Apple's Declared Age Range service for your age range, and you or your parent decide whether to share it. The App uses the answer only to meet those laws. It keeps the answer in memory while the App is open, never saves it, and never sends it anywhere. Crease is the same for every age.

9. Crash reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers (in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and statistics such as the number of installs and sessions. Apple provides these in a form that does not identify you to us. We use them only to find and fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

10. When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, any name in your email, your message and anything you attach. We use this only to reply to you, to help with your question and to keep a record of our conversation. Our email is hosted by Google, which processes it for us as our service provider. We keep support emails for up to two years after our last message, then delete them, unless we need to keep them longer to comply with the law or to establish or defend a legal claim. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

11. Children

Crease is made for readers of every age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App gives children no way to send us anything. If a child under 13 (or the age of digital consent where they live) emails us, we use their email address only to reply once to their request, we do not use it for anything else, and we delete it promptly after replying. Our retention policy for any information about a child is therefore to keep it only until that one reply is sent.

A parent or guardian who believes we have information about their child can email us to review it or have it deleted. Apple's Screen Time, Ask to Buy and Family Sharing give parents control over downloads and purchases.

12. When information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose the little we have only:

  • to our email provider, to store and deliver email for us;
  • when required by law, such as a valid court order;
  • when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us;
  • to someone who takes over the App, who must then keep this policy's protections.

13. Erasing your data

  • In the App: Settings › Privacy › Erase my progress removes your progress and settings from this device and from your iCloud copy, and, if you are signed in to Game Center, resets Crease's achievements. Your other devices pick up the erase through iCloud.
  • Deleting the App removes everything it stored on that device.
  • Purchases stay with your Apple Account, so you can restore them later.
  • Emails to us are deleted on request, or as described in Section 10.

14. Security

Information on your device is protected by iOS data protection and your passcode, and the iCloud copy is protected by Apple. We limit who can read support email and protect our accounts with strong authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but because we hold almost nothing about you, there is very little to protect.

15. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, to give you a copy, to correct it or to delete it. You can also object to or ask us to limit how we use it, and withdraw any consent you gave. In practice, the only personal information we may have about you is in emails you sent us.

To make a request, email berinshteyn@gmail.com. We may ask you to reply from the same address to confirm it is you. We will respond within 30 days, free of charge. You may use an authorized agent where your law allows. We will never treat you differently for using your rights.

If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with the word "Appeal". We will answer within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state Attorney General or your data protection authority.

16. Visitors from the European Union, the United Kingdom and elsewhere

We process personal information in support emails because we have a legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR and the UK GDPR), or to take steps you ask for, and to meet legal obligations. Emails are stored in the United States. Where the law requires, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension, or standard contractual clauses. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

17. U.S. state privacy rights

California. In the past 12 months, the only personal information we have received is identifiers (your email address) and the contents of messages, and only if you emailed us. It came from you, was used only to answer you, and was disclosed only to our email provider, as a service provider. We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information, and we keep information as described in Section 10. You have the rights to know, delete and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing.

Other states. Residents of states with consumer privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia, have similar rights, described in Section 15. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, so there is nothing to opt out of. We treat Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as a valid request to opt out anyway.

18. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is material, we will tell you in the App before it takes effect, and if the law requires your consent, we will ask for it.
