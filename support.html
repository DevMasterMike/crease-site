Support

Write to us at berinshteyn@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within a few days.

Restoring your purchase

Open Settings in the book (on the contents page, or from the pause menu) and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. With Family Sharing on, everyone in your family group gets every page too.

Free pages and "Every page, now"

Every page is free. Each one opens when you finish the one before it. If you would rather skip ahead, the optional "Every page, now" purchase opens them all at once.

Paper cranes and the shop

Each page hides three paper cranes. Spend them in the Stationer's Shop (in the pause menu, or the crane beside the Contents heading) on papers, hats and trails for Crease. Cranes are found only by playing: they cannot be bought and have no money value. Finding all 180 opens a secret page at the end of the last chapter.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced to your other iPhones and iPads. To erase it everywhere, open Settings › Privacy › Erase my progress.

Controls

On a foldable iPhone, fold the phone to open and close the book. Everywhere else, drag the bookmark ribbon at the edge of the page, or choose Tilt in Settings. Keyboards and game controllers work too, and every spread can be played with VoiceOver.
